Common questions

What does the app do?

It settles the "what do you want to do?" loop between two people. You each pick from a short set of options — food, something to watch, somewhere to go, an activity — and the app turns the overlap into one plan you both agreed to.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use it as a guest. An account exists so that two people can share the same decisions and plan across two phones, and so your history survives changing device.

How do I pair with my partner?

Both of you need the app; one shares the invite and the other accepts it. After that your decisions, plan, wishlist and journal are the same on both phones.

Why does it ask for my location?

Only to suggest places near you and to look up the weather where you are. You can decline and type a location instead — the rest of the app works either way.

Where do the film and restaurant suggestions come from?

Films and shows come from TMDB. Places come from Google Maps. Some ideas and the monthly recap are written by a language model from the context you picked. None of it is invented listings — if there is nothing to show, the app says so rather than filling the screen.

What does the subscription include, and how do I cancel?

The free version has limits on some features; the subscription lifts them. Purchases are handled by the App Store, so cancelling is done there: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals and leaves access running until the period you already paid for ends.

How do I delete my data?

The Profile screen has a Delete account action, available to guests as well as signed-in users. Deleting the app alone removes only the copy on the phone. You can also email the address above and we will delete it for you.

Something is wrong with a suggestion

Tell us what you saw and on which screen. Suggestions come from third-party catalogues that occasionally carry bad data, and knowing which one lets us filter it.

Privacy

What the app stores, and what it sends to Google, TMDB, OpenWeather and AI providers, is set out in the privacy policy.
