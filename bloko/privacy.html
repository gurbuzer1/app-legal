PRIVACY POLICY

BLOKO

Effective: 3 August 2026 · reviewed against the source 23 August 2026

BLOKO is a rune puzzle. It has no account, no server and no network code — this page is short because there is genuinely nothing to describe.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app makes no network requests of any kind. There is no sign-in, nothing to sync and no server that could receive anything even if we wanted it to.

What it writes to your device's own storage, and the whole of it:

  • your best score, your mana shards and your rank;
  • which schools you have unlocked and which one is in use;
  • your progress through the 99 trials — the stars earned on each one — and which chapter openings you have already seen;
  • the Daily Rite: today's best score, the day it was for, your streak and your best streak;
  • play statistics: games played, total score, lines, moves, best resonance, perfect clears, and how many spells and revives you have used;
  • your settings — sound, music, vibration and language;
  • whether you have been shown the tutorial, and the game you are in the middle of, so that closing the app does not throw a run away.

None of it identifies you, and none of it leaves the phone.

On iOS the game also keeps a copy of your progress in the app's own native storage. That is a safeguard against the system clearing the web view's storage and wiping a high score — it is still your device, and the copy is removed with the app.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. No analytics service, no advertising network, no crash reporter, no third-party SDK. Nothing counts how you play.

Purchases

There are none. Gems are earned by playing and cannot be bought; there is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no paid unlock. The + next to your gem count opens the Schools screen, not a shop.

Permissions

BLOKO asks for no permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photo library or notifications. The only device feature it touches is the haptic engine, for the tap you feel when a spell discharges.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything BLOKO has stored. Because none of it was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing for us to hold and nothing to request from us.

Children

BLOKO is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
