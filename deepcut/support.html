How to play

A category appears. Give a valid answer — and the more obscure your answer, the better you score. Low is good here: a famous answer costs you, and the rarest valid answer costs you nothing.

Lower is better?

Yes, and it is why your record starts empty rather than at zero. In a game where zero is perfect, a record initialised to zero would tell a player who has never played that their best is flawless — the most flattering possible lie, and one that could never be beaten. Until you finish a match the screens say so in words.

How is "obscure" decided?

By two published lists compiled into the app: country populations and English word frequencies. Nobody is surveyed, and no other player's answers affect your score. A "deep cut" is a round where nothing valid was rarer than what you gave.

The clock

Three settings: untimed, timed, and fast. Running out does not end the match — it passes the round. Untimed is the default because this is a thinking game.

Short game and long game

Five rounds is a bus stop; fifteen is the journey, and it keeps its own record because the two totals are not comparable. In this version both lengths are open.

Is there anything to buy?

Not in this version. If you ever see a length offered for sale and it does not appear after paying, Settings has a Restore purchases button; if that does not fix it, write to me.

Clearing your record

Settings can erase your record without touching your preferences, and without deleting the app.

Does it need a connection?

No. Every category is generated on your phone from data that ships inside the download. Aeroplane mode changes nothing.
