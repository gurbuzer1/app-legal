Privacy

Nerve · last updated 23 August 2026

Nerve collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it. The app makes no network requests at all. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, no in-app purchase, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores

Five numbers, on your own device, in the storage iOS gives the app for its own use: how many climbs you have finished, the most you have kept from one, the highest rung you have answered correctly, how many climbs you ended by walking away, and how many reached the top. That is the whole of it. Nothing in it identifies you, none of it is transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app removes it.

A climb in progress is deliberately not saved. Ladders are dealt from a seed and cost nothing to deal again, so there is nothing to keep.

What is in the app already

Every question is built on your phone from a country table compiled into the download: names, capitals, regions, land borders and currencies from the mledoze/countries dataset under the ODC Open Database License, and populations from World Bank indicator SP.POP.TOTL. Nothing is fetched while you play, which is why the game works with the aeroplane switch on — not as a fallback, but as the only way it works.

The numbers are not money

The ladder runs to 1,000,000 points. They are points. There is nothing to buy, no payment of any kind, and no relationship between the ladder and any currency.

Permissions

None are requested: no location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone, no photo library, no notifications, no identifier for advertisers. The app's configuration contains no usage-description key at all, because there is nothing to describe.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for children. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no browser, no external links inside the app, and nothing to buy. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your device: deleting the app deletes all of it.

Changes

If a future version of Nerve ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships.
