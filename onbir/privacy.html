PRIVACY POLICY

Onbir

Effective date: 27.07.2026

Onbir is a daily football-lineup puzzle. There is no account, no sign-in, and no personal profile. This page explains the one thing the app's server does keep, and the (larger) list of things it never asks for.

What is stored

An anonymous device identifier. On first launch the app generates a random id (a UUID) on your device and asks the server to remember it, matched with a signed cookie. The server uses this id to store exactly two things:

  • Your current daily streak (how many days in a row you've played)
  • Which day's puzzle you already submitted, and the squad you built for it — so the same device can't replay or re-roll a day it already finished

What is never collected

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind
  • No IP address or user-agent string is stored alongside your device id
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising or ad-tracking SDK
  • No analytics SDK from a third party
  • Nothing that links the device id back to who you are in real life

In Apple's App Privacy terminology: this app collects a device identifier used only for App Functionality (keeping your streak and preventing duplicate plays for the same day), it is not linked to your identity, and it is not used for tracking.

The player database

The football players you build your lineup from are described in the app's own rules page as plain text only — name, position, birth year, nationality and a one-line career note. No player photos, club crests or federation badges are used, now or ever. This data is hand-maintained by the developer; it is not licensed or scraped third-party media.

Sharing

When you share a finished lineup, the app generates a public "card" image (your squad, formation and the day's constraint) at a link you choose to share yourself. Anyone with that link can view the card; it contains no information about you beyond the squad you built.

Children's privacy

Onbir is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly target children. Since no personal information is ever collected, there is nothing to distinguish or remove for any age group.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
