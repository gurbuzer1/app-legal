Support

Onbir · last updated 23 August 2026

Anything at all — a player whose nationality or birth year is wrong, a constraint you believe is impossible, a lost streak, a crash — write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. For a data correction the player's name and what is wrong is enough. For a bug, your device model and iOS version gets it fixed much faster.

How to play

One constraint a day, the same one for everybody. Tap a position on the pitch, pick a player, fill all eleven slots without breaking the constraint, then lock your XI in. The formation changes with the day too, so the eleven slots are not always the same eleven positions.

The full rulebook is inside the app: tap the ? in the top right of the masthead. It also opens by itself the first time you play.

The constraint looks unfair

It is, on purpose. The job is not to build the best XI — it is to build the least bad XI under a bad rule. If the constraint were fair everybody would field the same eleven and there would be nothing to argue about. Nobody gets an easier one: the sentence is derived from the date, so your constraint today is everyone's constraint today.

The Lock button will not press

Then the squad still breaks the constraint, and the app says exactly which slots are at fault — offending players are marked on the pitch and the reason is listed above the button. Swap one of the marked players and the button lights up. If all eleven slots are full and no violation is listed, check your device's date: the card is built from today's date and a clock set to another day cannot produce one.

My streak did not go up

The streak is decided by the server, not by your phone, so that the same day cannot be counted twice. If the app could not reach the server when you locked in, the streak stays exactly where it was rather than counting a day it could not confirm — it does not silently invent one. Play again on a connection and it settles.

Days are counted in Europe/Istanbul time, so "today" rolls over at midnight Turkish time wherever you are.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The players, the constraints and the whole game are compiled into the app. With no connection you can still build your XI, lock it in and produce your card. The only things that need the network are the community numbers — how many people played today and how rare your XI was — and when they are unavailable the app simply does not show them rather than showing a broken panel.

Where is the archive of past days?

Your own boards are kept on the phone for seven days and then pruned; older days are not replayable. This is deliberate — Onbir is a today game, and a backlog of thirty unplayed constraints turns a two-minute habit into a chore.

Sharing a card

After you lock in, the card screen offers two ways out: share as a link, or share as an image. The link encodes the squad itself — anyone who opens it sees the eleven you picked and the constraint you picked them under, and nothing about you.

A player's facts are wrong

Write in and it gets fixed. The player table is plain text — name, position, birth year, nationality and a one-line career note — and it is maintained by hand, so it is exactly the kind of thing that carries mistakes. There are no player photos, club crests or federation badges in the app, now or ever.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything on the phone. On the server there is one anonymous row: a random device identifier with your streak and which days it played — no name, no email, no IP address, nothing that points back to you. To have that row removed, write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com.

Privacy

No account, no advertising, no third-party analytics. The full statement is at the privacy page.
