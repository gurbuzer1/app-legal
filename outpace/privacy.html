Privacy

Outpace · last updated 23 August 2026

Outpace collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it. The app makes no network requests at all. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores

Two things, both on your own device, in the storage iOS gives the app for its own use:

  • Your preferences — the appearance setting and whether the phone buzzes when you answer.
  • Your record — which pursuers you have got home against, your best prize, and how many matches you have finished.

Nothing in that list identifies you and none of it is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes it, and Settings can erase the record on its own without touching your preferences.

What is in the app already

Every question is built on your phone from data compiled into the download: country facts from the mledoze/countries dataset under the ODC Open Database License, World Bank populations, and public-domain English word lists. Nothing is fetched while you play, which is why the game works in aeroplane mode.

The prizes are not money

The figures in a match are fictional. No money changes hands, there is no gambling simulation, and nothing in the game can be exchanged for anything.

Permissions

None are requested: no location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone, no photo library, no notifications, no identifier for advertisers.

Purchases

There is nothing to buy in this version. The app links Apple's StoreKit and asks the App Store on launch whether an optional unlock is available; no such product exists yet, so nothing is offered, nothing is locked, and every pursuer is playable. If that unlock is ever published it will be a one-off, non-consumable purchase handled entirely by Apple: the app would never see your payment method, your Apple ID or your name, only whether the product is owned. Apple's own privacy policy governs any such transaction.

Children

Outpace is suitable for all ages. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no browser and no external links inside the app. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None beyond Apple's App Store. No advertising network, no analytics package, no crash reporter, no social sign-in, no cloud backend. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your device: deleting the app deletes all of it.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will say so plainly before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
