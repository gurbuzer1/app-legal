Shortlist — support

Last updated 23 August 2026 · gurbuzer1@gmail.com

Five hidden words. Ask for a letter, or answer. Everything below is how the game actually works, not how it is meant to feel.

The count

What is the big number?

How many words still fit everything you know. Before you ask anything it is 8,636 for a five-letter word — every five-letter word in the dictionary. Each letter you ask for cuts it down.

It is counted, not estimated, against all 46,977 playable words in the ENABLE dictionary. If it says 47, there are 47.

Why does asking for a letter that is not there still help?

Because "no E anywhere" rules out every word containing an E, which is often more words than a hit would rule out. A miss is not a wasted turn here — it is information, and the count moves to prove it.

The count did not move when I asked for a letter.

Then that letter told you nothing: every word still standing agreed about it. That can happen, and it is worth knowing — it means you spent a letter for nothing, which is exactly the kind of mistake the number exists to make visible.

The scoring

How are points worked out?

From how many words still fitted at the moment you answered — the doubt you removed yourself:

1,024 words fit100
47 words fit56
8 words fit30
2 words fit10
1 word fits0

Reveal until one word fits and there is nothing left to be right about — the game solved it and handed it to you.

I solved it and got zero.

Then only one word still fitted. That is not a bug and it is not a punishment: you are paid for the uncertainty you carried, and at that point there was none.

Why does a wrong answer end the word?

Because otherwise guessing at every step would be free, and the count would be decoration rather than a decision. The risk is what makes "answer now or ask again" a real question.

The words

Where do the words come from?

Two lists, doing two different jobs. The ENABLE word list decides what counts as a word: 172,823 words, of which the 46,977 that are five to seven letters long are playable here, and those are what the count is measured against. A frequency list decides what gets hidden: only the 4,000 most common words, so the answer is always something you could have known.

Both lists are public domain and compiled into the app before it ships.

I have never heard of a word it accepted as still fitting.

Likely true, and deliberate. The count has to be the truth about English rather than the truth about a trimmed list, so obscure words are counted even though they are never hidden. Telling you "12 words fit" while quietly meaning "12 words I judged you might know" would make the number worthless.

How long should a word take?

Measured over 400 words: typically 7 letters to be certain of, and nine in ten are pinned down inside 11. If you are regularly needing more than that, you are asking for letters that do not split the field — try one you expect about half the remaining words to have.

Those are percentiles rather than a smallest-and-largest, deliberately: the extremes of a sample move every time you take a different sample, and a number in a store listing that changes depending on which words you happened to check is not a measurement.

The app

Does it work offline?

It only works offline, in the sense that it never goes online at all. There is no network code in it. See the privacy page.

Is there anything to buy?

No. No paywall, no subscription, no adverts, nothing locked.

Can I play it with VoiceOver?

Yes. Every tile announces itself — "letter 1: P", "letter 2: blank" — and every key on the alphabet says which letter it asks for. If something is unreadable, please tell me; that is a bug worth fixing.

Can I reset my record?

Delete and reinstall the app. The record is a single small file in the app's own storage and goes with it.
