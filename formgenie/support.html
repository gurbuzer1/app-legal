Support

FormGenie · last updated 23 August 2026

Anything at all — an explanation that is wrong or out of date, a field the official form has and this one does not, a form you needed and could not find — write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. If it is about a field, the form's official id and the field label are enough for me to find it.

What it does

You pick an official form. FormGenie walks you through it one field at a time: what that field means, what to put in it, a worked example, and the mistakes people actually make on that particular field. At the end you see every answer on one screen, and a link to the real form so you can copy them across with the document checklist beside you.

Does it submit the form for me?

No. Nothing is filed, sent or submitted anywhere. FormGenie is a guide to the paper; the paper is still yours to file. That is also why it needs no account.

Does it fill in the PDF or export one?

No. There is no PDF generator in this app. An earlier version had a button that said "PDF export coming soon! Your answers have been saved." — the first half announced a feature that did not exist and the second half was untrue, because nothing was being saved at all. Both are gone. The review screen links to the official form instead, and the answers really are saved.

What languages is it in?

English only. Every screen, every field explanation and every worked example is in English, and there is no language switcher. The landing page used to print "7 Languages" as a headline figure; it was counting entries in a translation file that no screen ever displayed. That claim is gone rather than half-fixed.

Is there any AI in it?

No, despite the name it launched under. Every explanation is written text compiled into the app. No model is called, no API key exists, and nothing is sent anywhere to be interpreted.

Can I upload my own form?

Not yet, and the app no longer pretends otherwise. FormGenie covers a fixed library of 15 official forms across the US, the UK and India. If the form you need is not in the list, it is genuinely missing rather than hidden behind a feature.

It says the guide "does not cover" part of my form

That notice is deliberate and it is the most useful thing on the screen. Nine sections across the catalogue are part of the official form but are not modelled here — the W-4's "Step 5: Sign Here", GST REG-01's "Bank Account", and others. The wizard only walks through fields, so an unmodelled section used to be invisible: you could finish the app and believe you had finished the form. They are now named, with the official link underneath.

Are my answers saved?

Yes, on this device, as you type. Close the app mid-form and the answers are still there. They do not follow you to another phone, there is no backup, and deleting the app deletes them.

Is what I type private?

Completely. The app makes no network request of any kind, so tax and identity numbers you enter cannot leave the phone. The full statement is on the privacy page.

Does it need a connection?

No. The whole catalogue ships inside the app. The one thing that needs the internet is tapping through to an official government page, which opens your browser and leaves the app.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. FormGenie explains what fields mean. It does not know your circumstances and does not check your answers against them. For anything consequential, follow the official link or speak to a professional.
