PRIVACY POLICY

HabitForge

Effective: 1 August 2026

HabitForge tracks habits. It does that entirely on your phone, and this page is short because there is very little to describe.

What leaves your device

Nothing you enter. There is no account, no sign-in and no server behind this app. Habits, completions, streaks and settings are written to the app's own storage and stay there.

The single exception is a purchase: if you subscribe, the transaction is handled by Apple or Google, not by us. We never see your payment details — the store simply tells the app whether the subscription is active.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. No analytics service, no advertising network, no crash reporter, no third-party SDK of any kind. The app makes no network requests other than to the store when you open the subscription screen.

Permissions

HabitForge asks for no permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo library.

Deleting your data

Deleting a habit removes it. Deleting the app removes everything HabitForge has stored. Because none of it was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing for us to hold and nothing to request from us.

Children

HabitForge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
