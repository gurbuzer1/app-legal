Common questions

What does HabitForge do?

You list the habits you want to keep, tick them off as you do them, and it counts your streak. Every day it also shows one short, practical coaching tip — habit stacking, the two-day rule, how to restart after a break.

Is the coaching written by AI?

No. Earlier versions of this app called it "AI coaching"; that was wrong and has been removed. The tips are a fixed, hand-written set of habit-building advice. They are useful — they are just not generated, and the app no longer claims otherwise.

What does Premium give me?

Three things, and only things the app actually does: unlimited habits (the free tier tracks three), your full history — heatmap, charts and every past streak — and the whole coaching library rather than a tip a day.

It does not include cloud sync or backup. There is no server, so there is nothing to sync to, and we would rather say that than sell it.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or password.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to open the subscription screen. Tracking habits works entirely offline.

I subscribed on another device — how do I get it back?

Settings → Restore Purchases. Your subscription is tied to your Apple or Google account, so it follows you.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us: on iOS, Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops any further charge; the app keeps working on the free tier.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual habits in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Nothing is stored on a server, so there is nothing for us to delete.
