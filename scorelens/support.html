ScoreLens — Support

Updated: 3 August 2026

Have a problem, a question or a suggestion? Write directly to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. Replies usually arrive within a few business days. If a title was read wrongly, telling us what was on screen and what the app said helps far more than a screenshot of the error.

Common questions

What does it do?

Point the camera at a TV, laptop or phone showing a film or series — a poster, a detail page, a menu tile — and take a shot. ScoreLens reads the title from the picture and shows the rating, year, runtime and genre over the camera view, so you can keep browsing.

It did not recognise the title.

The frame has to actually contain a readable title. Recognition struggles when the title is only in artwork with no text, when the screen is at a sharp angle, or when motion blurs the shot. Hold still, fill more of the frame with the title area, and try again. Nothing is charged or counted for a failed read.

It named the wrong film.

It can happen with remakes, translated titles and series that share a name with a film. Write to us with what was on screen — those cases are the ones worth fixing.

Does it need internet?

Yes. Reading the title and looking up the rating both happen over the network, so ScoreLens will not work in aeroplane mode.

Do you keep my photos?

No. The single frame is used to read the title and then discarded — it is not stored by us or written to any database, and the app never touches your photo library. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is there an account or a subscription?

Neither. There is no sign-up, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

How do I clear my scan history?

It lives on your device only. Deleting the app removes it; there is nothing stored on our side to request or erase.
