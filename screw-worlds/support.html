Screw Worlds

Stuck on a puzzle?

If the waiting holes fill up, use an extra slot or an extra box, watch a short video for a free one, or try the puzzle again. You never lose progress in your homes, and every puzzle can be retried for free.

Bought a toolbox pack but don't see it?

Reopen the app: an unfinished purchase is delivered at the next launch.

Neighbourhood Pass or Quiet play on a new device?

Open Settings (gear icon) and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Ads

Reward videos only play when you choose them. After the first few puzzles a short ad may appear between puzzles; Quiet play or the Neighbourhood Pass turns those off. See the privacy policy for how ads use data.
