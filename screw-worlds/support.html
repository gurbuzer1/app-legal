Screw Worlds

Stuck on a puzzle?

If the waiting holes fill up, use an extra slot or an extra box, or try the puzzle again. You never lose progress in your homes, and every puzzle can be retried for free.

Bought a toolbox pack but don't see it?

Reopen the app: an unfinished purchase is delivered at the next launch.

Neighbourhood Pass on a new device?

Open Settings (gear icon) and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.
