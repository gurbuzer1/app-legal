PRIVACY POLICY

Screw Worlds

Effective: 9 November 2026

Screw Worlds is a single-player puzzle game. It has no account and no sign-in, and we run no server for it. This page describes what stays on your device, and the one thing that leaves it: anonymous play statistics. This version shows no ads.

What stays on your device

The game saves, on your device only:

  • your progress through the homes, rooms and objects, and the story choices you made;
  • your coins, the tools in your toolbox and the daily gift you last collected;
  • whether you own the Neighbourhood Pass or Quiet play;
  • your settings (sound effects, haptics, colour names).

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Anonymous play statistics (TelemetryDeck)

To learn where the game is too hard or confusing, the app sends short, anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service built for privacy. For example: a puzzle was started, finished, left part-way or got stuck; which screen was open when the app was closed; a tip was shown; an ad or purchase screen was used and with what result.

  • Each install gets a random number, which is scrambled (hashed) on your device before it is sent. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier.
  • No progress saves, story choices, text you type, contacts, photos or precise location are sent.
  • The data is used only to improve the game. It is not sold and not used for advertising or tracking.

See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy. Deleting the app removes the random number; a new install starts with a new one.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

This version of the game shows no ads, and Google's ad software in the app stays switched off, so it collects nothing. If a later update turns ads on, we will update this page first, and what follows will apply.

The free game shows optional reward videos (you choose to watch one, for example for an extra slot) and, after the first few puzzles, an occasional short ad between puzzles. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) may be inferred;
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data such as ad load errors;
  • the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices once ads are on

None of this applies to this version, which shows no ads and never asks these questions.

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Screw Worlds may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • Consent (UK, EEA, Switzerland and other regions where it is required): a Google consent form asks how your data may be used for ads before any ad is requested.
  • No ads between puzzles: the one-time Quiet play purchase, or the Neighbourhood Pass, turns off the ads between puzzles. Reward videos stay available only if you choose to watch them.

Purchases

The Neighbourhood Pass, Quiet play and the toolbox packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only stores, on your device, what you own. Tap Restore purchase in Settings to recover one-time purchases on a new device.

Permissions

Screw Worlds never uses your camera, microphone, contacts, location services or photo library. Besides the tracking question above, it asks for no permissions.

Children

Screw Worlds is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
