Common questions

What does FitSnap actually do?

You photograph your finger, foot or waist with an everyday object beside it for scale, and FitSnap works out your ring, shoe or waist size — in US, EU, UK and Japanese sizing, with notes on how well-known brands tend to run.

Why do I need a coin or a card in the photo?

A photo on its own has no sense of scale. The reference object is a known real-world size, so the app can convert pixels into millimetres. Without it, no measurement is possible — this is also why the object has to lie flat, in the same plane as what you are measuring.

How accurate is it?

It is an estimate, and it says so: every result comes with a range, not just a single number. Good light, a sharp photo and a flat reference object make the range tighter. For an engagement ring or anything expensive, confirm with a physical sizer first.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or password, and your sizes are not synced anywhere.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Measuring works entirely offline.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos are analysed on your phone and are not kept after the measurement is taken. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Clear your measurement history from the Profile screen, or delete the app to remove everything. Nothing is stored on a server, so there is nothing for us to delete.
