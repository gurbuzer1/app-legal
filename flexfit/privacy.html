Privacy

FlexFit Tracker · last updated 23 August 2026

FlexFit Tracker collects nothing and has no server to send it to. Your exercises, templates, workouts and records are written to your own phone and stay there. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising network, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores, and where

Everything lives in the app's own private storage on your device, as plain JSON files plus a few small settings:

  • Exercises — the preset library and any you create: name, muscle group, type.
  • Templates — the routines you build out of those exercises.
  • Workout logs — each session: which exercises, the sets, reps and weights you entered, when it started and how long it took.
  • Personal records — your best weight, best reps and best volume per exercise, and when you hit them.
  • Settings — theme, kilograms or pounds, default rest timer, and whether you have seen the introduction.

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no backup on any server, because there is no server.

What leaves the device

Nothing. The app makes no network request — not at startup, not while you train, not in the background. The one outward door is Send Feedback, which opens your mail app with a message addressed to me; nothing is attached, and whatever you type is all I see.

Permissions

None. The app asks for no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no photo library, no health data and no notifications. An earlier build declared tracking permission wording meant for advertising; there has never been an ad in this app and the declaration has been removed.

Advertising, tracking and purchases

There are none of the first two. An earlier store description said "this app contains ads" and the code carried an unfinished advertising stub; no advertising library has ever actually been part of the app, and the claim has been removed rather than left standing. There is likewise nothing for sale inside the app: no subscription, no unlock, no price is shown anywhere, and every feature is available to everybody.

Health data

The app does not read from or write to Apple Health, and does not ask to. The numbers it holds are the ones you type into it.

Children

There is no chat, no user-generated content from anyone else, no browser and nothing to buy. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your training log could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Not medical or coaching advice

FlexFit Tracker records what you did. It does not prescribe programmes, judge your form, or know anything about your health. Train sensibly and take advice from someone qualified.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your phone: deleting the app deletes all of it.
