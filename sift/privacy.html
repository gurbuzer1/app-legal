Privacy

Sift · last updated 23 August 2026

Sift collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it. The app makes no network requests of its own. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could add one. The single exception is Apple's own StoreKit, used only to ask whether the one optional purchase has been made.

What the app stores

Five things, all of them on your own device, in the storage iOS gives the app for its own use:

  • Your settings — the appearance you chose, whether haptics are on, and how many wrong guesses a wall allows.
  • Your statistics — boards played, boards solved, how many you cleared without a mistake, your current run and your best run.
  • The board you left half-played, so closing the app mid-puzzle does not lose it. This is stored as the board's number plus which tiles are still on it — the puzzle itself is rebuilt on your phone from that number.
  • Which board comes next.
  • Whether you bought "Naming the rule", so the feature keeps working with no connection. This is a yes-or-no flag and nothing else.

Nothing in that list identifies you, and none of it is transmitted anywhere. It is removed when you delete the app.

What is in the app already

The country table and the word list ship inside the download — country facts from the mledoze/countries dataset under the ODC Open Database License, and public-domain English word lists. Puzzles are generated on your phone, which is why the game works in aeroplane mode; that is not a fallback, it is the only way it works.

Permissions

None are requested: no location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone, no photo library, no notifications, no identifier for advertisers.

The one purchase

Sift has one optional, non-consumable in-app purchase, "Naming the rule". It is handled entirely by Apple: the app never sees your payment method, your Apple ID, or your name. All it receives back is whether the product is owned. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.

Nothing is taken away to create it. Every board, unlimited play, and the full reveal at the end of a board are free and stay free.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for children. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no browser, and no external links inside the app. The one optional purchase uses the device's normal purchase controls — including Screen Time restrictions, if you have set them. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None beyond Apple's App Store. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend. The app has no server component of its own, so there is no operator to whom your data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your device and under your control: deleting the app deletes all of it.

Changes

If a future version of Sift ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than buried in a version bump.
