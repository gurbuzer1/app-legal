Support

Sift · last updated 23 August 2026

Anything at all — a grouping you believe is wrong, a board that looked impossible, a crash, a purchase that did not restore — write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. If it is about a board, the four tiles you picked are enough for me to find it.

How to play

Sixteen tiles, four hidden rules, four tiles each. Pick four you think share a rule and submit. Wrong guesses are limited; how many is yours to set in Settings.

I picked four things that clearly go together and it said no

Then they share a rule that is not one of this board's four. Sift says so rather than just buzzing:

Those do share a rule — just not one of this board's four.

That is the difficulty on purpose. Some tiles fit two rules, and only one grouping of all sixteen works.

"One away"

Three of your four belong to one rule and the fourth does not. It tells you the shape of the mistake without telling you which tile is the intruder.

What "Naming the rule" adds

When four tiles you pick share a rule that is not on the board, Sift already tells you so. The purchase adds the next sentence: which rule it was. That is all it does. Every board, unlimited play, and the full reveal of all four rules at the end of a board are free and stay free.

It is a one-off, non-consumable purchase. If you have bought it before, Settings has a Restore purchases button.

Nothing about the purchase appears on my screen

That is deliberate, not a failure to load. The unlock is only ever offered when the App Store confirms it is actually for sale. When the store cannot be reached — offline, signed out, or a sandbox where the product does not resolve — the app shows nothing about it at all rather than a padlock you cannot open.

Are the boards fair?

Every board is checked before it is dealt, not promised. The grouping of all sixteen tiles has to be the only one that works, and a board that fails is discarded and another is drawn.

I closed the app mid-board

It comes back. The board is stored as its number and the tiles still on it, and rebuilt on your phone from that number. If a future version changes how boards are generated, the saved board would no longer describe a real board — so the app re-derives it on load and throws the save away rather than showing you something that never existed.

Does it need a connection?

No. Everything happens on your phone; the word list and country table ship inside the download. Aeroplane mode changes nothing except that the store cannot be asked about the optional purchase.
