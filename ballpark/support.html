Ballpark — support

Last updated 22 August 2026 · gurbuzer1@gmail.com

Eight questions, no clock. You slide to a number and commit. Everything below is how the game actually works, not how it is meant to feel.

The scoring

How is my guess scored?

By the factor you were out by, never the difference:

exactly right100
within 5%98
within 10%96
out by 2×70
out by 3×52
out by 5×30
out by 10× or more0

It makes no difference whether the answer was eleven thousand or a billion. Being out by three is being out by three.

Why not just score the difference?

Because it produces nonsense. Guessing two million when the answer is one million and guessing 101 million when the answer is 100 million are both "a million out" — but the first is twice the right answer and the second is essentially correct. Worse: a guess a thousand times too big on a small country is a smaller difference than a guess one percent off on a large one, so difference-based scoring would call the wild guess the better of the two.

I guessed too low and got the same score as someone who guessed too high.

Yes, deliberately. Twice the answer and half the answer are the same size of error. The televised format this comes from kills you for going over and forgives you for going under; that is good television and a poor measure of what anyone knows.

The slider

Why does the slider move so fast at the top?

It does not — it moves at the same rate everywhere, and the rate is a ratio. Every point of travel multiplies the number by the same amount, which is why the marks underneath are 1, 10, 100, 1k and so on rather than evenly spaced numbers. A linear slider across a billion would spend nine tenths of its travel on the last order of magnitude and squeeze every small answer into a few pixels.

I cannot get the exact number I want.

The slider resolves to about 8% per point of travel on the smallest phone, which is far finer than anyone's intuition about the area of Chad — and a guess 8% out still scores 96. If you find a case where that costs you a point you had earned, please say so.

Does the slider's range give the answer away?

It is not allowed to. The range is a property of the kind of question and is identical for every country, and every one of the 768 answers is checked to sit well away from both ends. Monaco failed that check while it was being built — 2km² sat too near the start of a slider beginning at 1km² — and the range was widened.

The questions

Where do the numbers come from?

Population and GDP per capita from the World Bank; area and country names from mledoze/countries under the Open Database Licence. Population density is those two divided, and says so. Nothing is typed by hand — every figure is compiled from a source file before the app ships, which is why each answer can show you where it came from.

Why does it show a year?

Because a figure without one is not a fact. The World Bank's most recent GDP per capita is 2025 for most countries and as old as 2011 for a few, so "GDP per capita in Syria is $1,266" is false while "in 2011 it was $1,266" is true.

Some questions feel easier than others.

They are, and it is measured. A player who knows nothing and always gives the same answer scores 38 out of 100 on area, 43 on population, 49 on GDP and 58 on density. So every round deals two of each kind rather than picking at random — otherwise some rounds would be markedly easier for a reason you could not see.

An entire question type was cut for failing that test: "how far apart are two countries" scored 74.8 for someone who always answers 7,630km, because nearly every pair of countries is between four and twelve thousand kilometres apart.

A fact in a question is wrong.

Please tell me which one. Every figure is compiled from a source rather than typed, so an error is either upstream or in how the question was built — and both are worth fixing.

The app

Does it work offline?

It only works offline, in the sense that it never goes online at all. There is no network code in it. See the privacy page.

Is there anything to buy?

No. No paywall, no subscription, no adverts, nothing locked.

Can I reset my record?

Delete and reinstall the app. The record is a single small file in the app's own storage and goes with it; nothing is kept anywhere else.
