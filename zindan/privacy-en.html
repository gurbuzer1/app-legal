Privacy policy

Updated 14 October 2026

Your adventure is saved on your device. Dungeon can be played without an account or an internet connection. The game does not send your saved progress to a developer-operated server. This release has no advertising or in-app purchasing flow.

What stays on your device

The game saves your selected hero, coins, ingredients and their quality, earned equipment levels, records and current expedition. A checkpoint also contains the run identifier and seed, elapsed game time, room state, enemies, projectiles, upgrades and meal effects needed to resume play. Sound preferences are stored locally too.

The current profile uses dungeon-roomrun-v1.json. Temporary and backup files and a local history folder help recover interrupted or damaged saves. If an older save is available, the game preserves it and can keep an unfinished classic run separately from a new expedition. This means several local recovery copies may exist, not just one save file.

Analytics, ads and permissions

This release does not enable advertising, gameplay analytics or developer cloud crash-reporting services. It does not ask you to provide an account, contacts, location, camera, microphone or photo-library access. Unity is the game engine; installed engine packages alone are not a statement that every optional Unity service is active.

Links you choose to open

Choosing Privacy Policy or Help & Support opens a website in your browser. That action uses an internet connection. Our pages are hosted by Vercel: your browser sends connection information, including your IP address and request headers, to the host to retrieve the page. We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or cookies to these pages.

If you email support, we receive the address and content you choose to send, including any attachments. We use those messages to respond and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Support correspondence is separate from the local game save.

Retention, backups and deletion

Progress and recovery copies remain in the app’s local storage; old recovery files are not automatically rotated away. There is no in-game account or cloud-save service to delete. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its installed local data. Device or iCloud backups may retain a copy under your Apple backup settings; manage those separately. We cannot restore a local save that is no longer available.

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, diagnostic or device-backup information under your Apple settings and its own policies. Those platform services are separate from developer-operated game services.

For help with the game, questions about a message you sent, or a request to delete that correspondence, email the support address below. Include enough context to identify your request, without sending passwords or other sensitive information.

Children and changes

The same data practices apply to all players. The game has fantasy combat; the App Store’s age rating describes its content. If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its date.
