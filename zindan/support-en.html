Dungeon · Zindan: Kes ve Pişir

Help & support

Updated 7 November 2026

Pick a hero, fight down through the dungeon and cook what the monsters drop. These notes describe the current game: five heroes and two chapters of 33 chambers each.

Move, stop, attack

Drag anywhere on the play area to move. Lift your finger to stop: your hero attacks the nearest target it can reach on its own. Move again to step out of an incoming attack. Cover blocks shots, so reposition when there is no clear target. There is no separate attack or dodge button.

Five heroes

  • Butcher: a thrown cleaver that cuts again on its way back.
  • Mage: magic bolts that pierce enemies.
  • Baker: dough balls that arc over cover; flour slows foes.
  • Pantry Thief: a fan of daggers; snap-traps hold foes.
  • Monster Taster: a giant fork; every new kind of monster tasted makes it hit harder.

All five heroes are available from the start. Choose one on the Home screen; the choice applies to your next run.

Two chapters, four guardians each

Chapter 1, The Hungry Halls, is open from the start. Clearing it unlocks chapter 2, The Smokehouse. Each chapter has 33 chambers in four wings. A wing mixes ordinary fights, an elite chamber and a rush chamber where a second wave pours in, then a quiet kitchen and the wing’s guardian. Kitchens are chambers 7, 15, 23 and 32; guardians wait in chambers 8, 16, 24 and 33. Watch the warnings on the floor before you commit to attacking.

Each time your hero levels up during a run, choose one of three powers. Powers last for that run only.

Cooking

Monsters drop ingredients: meat, bone, hide, sinew, sap and crystal. Elites and guardians are the best source of fine-quality ingredients. In a kitchen, tap COOK and pick one of seven recipes; each shows its cost and effects. A finished meal restores health and gives its bonus for the next three encounters. A new meal replaces the previous one; meals do not stack. Kitchens are safe, and leaving before the meal is ready does not use your ingredients.

Between runs

When a run ends in defeat or victory, the gold and unused ingredients you carried come home. At Home:

  • Workbench: spend gold on weapon (damage) and apron (health) levels. Your account level, earned by playing, raises the workbench limit.
  • Shop: workbench goods, paid for with gold earned in the dungeon.
  • Gear (on the Workbench): three matching pieces merge into a stronger one.
  • Pantry: pack up to 24 of each ingredient for your next run.

Gold packs (optional)

Tap + next to your gold at the top of Home (also on Workbench and Pantry) to open GOLD PACKS. Pouch of gold (600), Chest of gold (1,800) and Vault of gold (5,000) are one-time consumable purchases made through the App Store; your first purchase on a save pays double gold once. Every chamber, hero and chapter can be played without buying anything.

Purchased gold is added to the save on this device. Because packs are consumable, they cannot be restored after the app is deleted. If a purchase did not arrive, write to us with the date and the pack name. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Saving

The game saves at room transitions, after important choices and when iOS sends it to the background. A run in progress survives switching apps. If the app is closed completely during a run, that run ends: the supplies you packed return to the pantry, but what the run picked up is lost. If the game cannot load or save safely, it shows a message and keeps the existing files instead of replacing them. Please contact us with that message, and do not delete the app while trying to recover progress.

Settings and reminders

Settings (gear icon on Home, or Pause) has music, sound effects, haptics, frame rate, battle tips and reminders. Reminders are off unless you turn them on; iOS then asks for notification permission. Menus are in English. Privacy Policy and Help & Support open a browser only when you tap them; gameplay works offline.

When you write, include your device model, iOS version, the game version shown in Settings, your chamber and what happened. Settings has a button that copies these details for you. A screenshot helps. We cannot see your local save remotely.
