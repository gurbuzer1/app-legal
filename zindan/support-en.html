Dungeon · Zindan: Kes ve Pişir

Help & support

Updated 14 October 2026

Choose your hero, fight through the dungeon and turn the ingredients you collect into your next advantage. These instructions describe the new 25-chamber expedition. An unfinished older 20-room run keeps its original rules.

Move, stop, attack

Drag on the play area to move. Release your finger to stop and automatically attack a target your hero can reach. Move again to leave an incoming attack. Cover can block shots, so reposition when your hero has no clear target. There is no separate swing or dodge-roll button.

Choose Archer for arrows, Butcher for a returning cleaver, or Mage for piercing magic. The Butcher’s throw is deliberate: let the cleaver return and look for a safe opening. A hero choice applies to a new run, not to the hero already inside a saved run.

Five districts, five guardians

A new expedition contains 25 chambers across five districts. Each district has three combat rooms, a quiet kitchen and a guardian. Room layouts change between runs, and each district brings different enemy groups. Watch the warnings on the floor and the guardian’s movements before committing to an attack.

Clear every wave to open the gold exit, choose a power when offered, then walk through the gate. Powers selected during a run last for that run.

Cooking and ingredients

Kitchens appear in chambers 4, 9, 14, 19 and 24. Open the cooking menu there and use Previous / Next to browse all seven recipes. The recipe shows its ingredient cost, any fine-quality requirement and its effects. Elites and guardians are sources of fine ingredients.

A finished meal restores some health and grants benefits for the next three combat encounters. A new meal replaces the previous meal’s benefits; they do not stack. Kitchens are safe. Cooking takes a few seconds; cancelling or leaving before completion does not consume the ingredients. There is no weapon durability or repair system in this expedition.

What carries into the next run?

When a run ends in defeat or victory, its coins and unused ingredients return to your pantry. Earned weapon and apron upgrades remain and strengthen new expeditions. A new expedition starts in chamber 1; that run’s level, selected powers and meal effects start over.

Use Workbench at home to spend earned coins on damage or maximum health. Your backpack takes a limited supply from storage when a new expedition begins; surplus ingredients remain in the home pantry. The current game has no ads or real-money purchase screen.

Pause, save and continue

Pause the game or choose Save & Return Home before taking a break. The game also saves at room transitions, after important choices and when it receives an app pause or focus-loss event. It avoids periodic disk writes during active combat. Abrupt device shutdown can still lose changes since the last successful save.

Continue resumes the saved run. Local backup and history copies can help recover a damaged save. If the game cannot safely load or save progress, it displays an error and protects the existing files instead of silently replacing them. Please contact support with the message shown; do not delete the app while trying to recover progress.

What happens to an older run?

You can continue an unfinished saved 20-room run with its original rules. If you enter the new expedition first, that classic run is put aside rather than erased. After the current expedition ends, Return to Your Saved Run can restore it. The original older Dungeon save is preserved when its coins, ingredients and supported damage and health upgrades are imported.

Language, sound and help

The current game menus and instructions are in English. This support page is available in English and Turkish. Sound can be switched off in Pause. Privacy Policy and Help & Support open a browser only when you choose them; those pages need internet access, while gameplay does not.

For help, include your device model, iOS version, game build number if known, chamber and what happened. A screenshot or short recording can help, but send only what you are comfortable sharing. We cannot access your local save remotely.
