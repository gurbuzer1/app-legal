Common questions

What does CanIAI actually do?

You describe an idea, and CanIAI gives you an honest read on whether it is feasible — including the reasons it might not be. It is built to tell you "no" when "no" is the right answer, rather than encouraging every idea.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or password.

Does it need an internet connection?

Yes. Your question is sent to a server to be answered, so the app needs to be online to give you a result.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. If you would like anything held server-side removed as well, email the address above and say so — no account details are needed.
