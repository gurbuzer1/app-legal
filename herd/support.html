HERD — Support

Updated: 5 September 2026

Have a problem, a question or a suggestion? Write directly to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. Replies usually arrive within a few business days. If you are reporting a bug, including your device model and iOS version gets it fixed much faster.

Common questions

How do I play?

You pick one direction and everyone in the herd walks it together. Press up and every member tries to take one step up at the same moment. Get all of them onto their own homes and the level is solved.

Then how do they ever separate?

Walls. A wall stops whoever runs into it, but the others carry on — so one press can move some of the herd and not the rest, and a gap opens. Opening those gaps in the right order is the whole game.

Which home belongs to which character?

Each home shows a faded picture of the member it belongs to, so you can read it straight off the board. The names are listed under the board as well.

I am stuck.

Press HELP. It works out the shortest remaining solution from where you actually are and tells you the next direction. If the position can no longer be won, it says so plainly instead of letting you wander — undo, or restart the level.

Is my score compared with anyone else's?

No. The "best possible" number is not another player — it is the shortest solution that exists for that level, proved by the game itself. Matching it means nobody could have done it in fewer moves.

Does it work offline?

Always. The game never makes a network request, so aeroplane mode changes nothing. There are no ads and no purchases.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and there is no account or backup to restore it from.
