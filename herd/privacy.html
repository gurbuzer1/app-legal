PRIVACY POLICY

HERD

Effective: 5 September 2026

HERD is a puzzle game about a small herd that all walks in the same direction. It has no account, no server and no network code — this page is short because there is genuinely nothing to describe.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app makes no network requests of any kind. There is no sign-in, nothing to sync, and no server that could receive anything even if we wanted it to.

What is stored, and where

Two things are written to your device's own storage and stay there:

  • Your progress — which levels you have solved and the move sequence you solved them with, so the game can show your best result and unlock the next level.
  • Your sound preference — whether sound is on or off.

That is the complete list. There is no profile, no identifier, no device fingerprint and no usage history. Nothing here is personal information, and none of it is readable by anyone but you.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. No analytics service, no advertising network, no crash reporter, no third-party SDK. Nothing counts how you play.

Purchases

There are none. The game is free, all 99 levels are included, and there is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no paid unlock. Levels are unlocked by solving the earlier ones — that is the only key, and it cannot be bought.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes both stored values with it. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing left to request, export or erase.

Children

HERD is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever stores or sends something new, this page changes in the same release — not after it.
