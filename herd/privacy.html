PRIVACY POLICY

Move the Herd

Effective: 12 October 2026 · updated in the same release as version 1.1

Move the Herd is a puzzle game about a small herd that all walks in the same direction. It has no account and no server of ours — this page is short because there is genuinely little to describe.

What leaves your device

Nothing we can read. The game itself makes no network requests: there is no sign-in, nothing to sync, and no server of ours that could receive anything even if we wanted it to.

There are exactly two exceptions. Both are Apple's rather than ours, and neither reaches us.

1. The unlock. When you tap Unlock or Restore, Apple's own purchase system (StoreKit) talks to Apple to take the payment and to tell the app whether this device owns the unlock. That exchange is between you and Apple. We are not part of it and we never see it — see Purchases below.

2. The leaderboard, only if you ask for it. Tapping Leaderboard in Settings opens Apple's Game Center. From then on the game sends Game Center one number — your total stars — so it can rank you. That is the only value sent, and it is sent to Apple, not to us. Your Game Center name, friends and player identifier stay with Apple: the app asks Game Center only whether you are signed in, and the answer it receives is yes or no. If you never tap Leaderboard, Game Center is never contacted, and nothing else in the game requires it. Apple's handling of Game Center is described in Apple's Game Center privacy notice.

What is stored, and where

These eight values are written to your device's own storage and stay there. This is the complete list:

  • Your classic progress — which of the 236 classic levels you have solved and the move sequence you solved them with, so the game can show your best result and open the next level.
  • Your progress in the new rules — the same thing for the six season chapters, the Daily Herd and Endless, kept as a separate value.
  • Your crystals — how many hints you can still afford.
  • How many hints you have used — a counter kept from earlier versions, so an update does not hand you hints you already spent.
  • Your sound preference — on or off.
  • Your haptics preference — on or off.
  • Whether you have opened the leaderboard — a single yes/no, so the game knows to sign you in to Game Center quietly next time instead of asking again.
  • Whether this device owns the unlock — a cached yes/no. The real answer lives with Apple; this copy only saves asking on every launch.

There is no profile, no identifier, no device fingerprint and no usage history. None of it is personal information, and none of it is readable by anyone but you. Deleting the app deletes all eight.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. No analytics service, no advertising network, no attribution or marketing SDK, no crash reporter, no tracking of any kind. Nothing counts how you play.

To be precise rather than merely reassuring: the app is built on third-party open-source code, and here is all of it. React and React Native for Web draw the screens. Capacitor packages the app for iOS and provides the haptics (the small tap when a move lands). Cordova Plugin Purchase is the bridge to Apple's StoreKit, used only when you tap Unlock or Restore. The Game Center bridge is our own code. None of these collects, transmits or stores anything about you, and none of them is an analytics, advertising or attribution library.

Purchases

The first 15 classic levels are free, along with the Spring chapter, the Daily Herd and challenge codes. Unlocking the remaining 221 classic levels, the five other chapters, Endless, Reverse, the level editor and unlimited hints is a single one-time purchase; there is no subscription and nothing is consumable. Crystals are earned by playing and are not for sale.

That purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card, your Apple ID, or your name — the app only ever learns whether this device owns the unlock, and that answer is cached on the device like everything else here. There is no account and no server, so there is nothing for us to link a purchase to.

Progress itself still cannot be bought. A level opens because you solved the one before it; the purchase removes the price, not the puzzle.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes every stored value with it. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing left to request, export or erase. Your purchase is the one thing that survives, because Apple — not we — remembers it; Settings → Restore purchase asks Apple for it again. Any score you posted to Game Center belongs to your Game Center account, and Apple's own settings control it.

Children

Move the Herd is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever stores or sends something new, this page changes in the same release — not after it.
