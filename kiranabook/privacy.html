Privacy

KiranaBook · last updated 23 August 2026

KiranaBook collects nothing and has no server to send it to. Your customers, your khata, your stock and your bills are written to your own phone and stay there. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising network, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is kept in the app's own private storage on your device, in five local databases:

  • Customers — the name and phone number you type for each khata customer.
  • Khata entries — every credit and payment you record, with its date and note.
  • Products — your catalogue: name, category, prices, stock, and the barcode number if you type one in.
  • Bills — the bills you create, their line items, GST and totals.
  • Shop settings — your shop name, address, phone, GSTIN and UPI ID, and your language and theme choice.

Deleting the app removes all of it. Nothing is backed up to us, because there is no "us" on a server anywhere.

What leaves the device — only when you press the button

KiranaBook never sends anything on its own. It makes no network request in the background, at startup, or while you work. There are four places where you can hand something to another app, and each one is a deliberate tap:

  • Share or print a bill. The bill is rendered as a PDF on your phone and passed to the iOS share sheet or to AirPrint. Where it goes next is whatever you pick there.
  • WhatsApp reminder. Tapping the reminder opens WhatsApp with the message already written. Nothing is sent until you send it, and it goes through WhatsApp, not through us.
  • SMS reminder. The same, through your phone's Messages app.
  • UPI QR code. The QR image is drawn on your phone from the UPI ID you typed. Scanning it is a payment between your customer and their payment app; the app never sees the payment.

Permissions

None. The app asks for no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no photo library and no notifications. An earlier build declared camera access for a barcode scanner that was never built; the declaration has been removed, because a permission request you cannot explain is worse than a missing feature.

Advertising and tracking

There are none. Earlier versions of this document, and a note inside the app itself, described advertising through Google AdMob collecting device identifiers. That was never true: no advertising library has ever been part of this app, and the claim has been removed rather than left standing. KiranaBook shows no ads, uses no advertising identifier, and does not ask to track you across apps.

Children

KiranaBook is a tool for running a shop, not a children's app, but nothing here is unsafe for one. There is no chat, no browsing, no user-generated content from strangers and nothing to buy. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend, no payment processor. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your shop's data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under India's DPDP Act, the UK and EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your phone: deleting the app deletes all of it.
