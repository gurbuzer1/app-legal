Support

Anything at all — a total that came out wrong, a khata balance you do not recognise, a bill that would not print, a crash — write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. Tell me what you tapped and what you expected; that is usually enough to find it.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone, in the app's own storage, and nowhere else. There is no account and no cloud. That is deliberate — the shop's book is yours — but it has one consequence worth saying plainly: if you delete the app or lose the phone, the data goes with it. There is no server copy to restore from.

Does it need internet?

No. Billing, khata, stock, reports and the UPI QR code all work in aeroplane mode. The only moments a connection matters are the ones you start yourself: opening WhatsApp to send a reminder, or sharing a bill to another app.

A customer's balance looks wrong

Open the customer and read down the entries. The balance is a running total: every credit adds, every payment subtracts, and deleting an entry puts back exactly what it took. That agreement is not assumed — the test suite adds long runs of mixed entries, deletes some, closes and reopens the book, and checks the stored balance still equals credit minus payments for the rows you can see. If your rows are right and the total is not, please write to me with the figures; that is a bug and I want it.

Can I scan a barcode with the camera?

Not yet. The barcode field on a product is typed in, and it is used for searching your own catalogue. The app asks for no camera permission, because it has no scanner to use it with.

Is the GST calculation official?

No. The GST percentage is one you enter, and the app multiplies. It is arithmetic to save you doing it on paper, not tax advice — check anything that goes to your accountant.

Are there ads or a subscription?

Neither. Every feature in the app is available to everybody, there is nothing to buy inside it, and no advertising library has ever been part of it.

Hindi and English

Both are complete. Switch in Settings → Language; the change applies immediately and is remembered.

Terms of use

KiranaBook is provided free, as is, with no warranty. It is a supplementary tool for running a shop, not an accounting system of record. In particular:

  • Bill totals and GST figures are arithmetic on the numbers and percentages you enter. Check anything that goes to your accountant or to the tax authorities.
  • Khata balances are what the app has been told. They are not proof of a debt.
  • The UPI QR code is drawn from the UPI ID you typed. Payments happen between your customer and their payment app; the app is not a payment processor and never sees the money.

Your shop's data lives only on your device. There is no server copy, so keeping it is your responsibility: if the phone is lost, reset, or the app deleted, the book goes with it and cannot be recovered by me or by anyone else.

There is nothing to buy inside the app and no advertising. Questions about these terms go to gurbuzer1@gmail.com, the same address as everything else.
