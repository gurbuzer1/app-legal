Privacy

Beraber (HERD) · last updated 23 August 2026

Beraber collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it. The app makes no network requests. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores

One thing, on your own device, in the storage the app is given for its own use: for each level you have solved, the sequence of directions that solved it.

A saved solution is literally a short string of letters, one per step — up, down, left, right — filed under the level it belongs to. That is the entire save. There is no name, no timestamp, no device identifier, no play time, no score, and no record of the attempts that did not work.

The save is not a claim the app trusts. Each time a level is checked, the stored sequence is walked again on that level's board; if it does not actually finish the level, it does not count. That is why there is no "solved" flag to store — the app can always tell by replaying what you did.

Nothing in the save identifies you, none of it is transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app removes it.

Where the puzzles come from

All ninety-nine boards are built into the app before it ships, and every one of them was solved by computer first. Nothing is fetched while you play, which is why the game works with the aeroplane switch on.

Permissions and money

The app requests no permissions at all — not the camera, the microphone, your location, your photos, your contacts, or notifications. It does not ask because it has no code that would use them. There are also no in-app purchases, no subscription and no advertising: the app links no payment or ad framework of any kind, so there is nothing to bill and no payment detail for it to see.

Children

The app is safe for children. There is no chat, no user-generated content shared with anyone, no browser, no link that leaves the app, and nothing to buy. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your device: deleting the app deletes all of it.
