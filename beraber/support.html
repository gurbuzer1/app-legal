Beraber (HERD) · last updated 23 August 2026

Write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. A real person reads it. Tell me your device, your iOS version, and which level you were on — that is usually enough to find the problem.

The game

You pick one direction and everyone in the herd walks it together. Each member has a home of its own, and the level is done when every one of them is standing on theirs.

Why they end up in different places

Because walls stop them one at a time. When you choose a direction, four things can happen to each walker on that step: it moves one square, it walks into the edge and stays put, it walks into a wall and stays put, or it gets blocked by the one in front. Whoever is stopped falls behind, and that is how a herd that started as one clump spreads out into the shape you need.

Undo

One wrong direction drags the whole herd somewhere you did not want, so undo takes the last step back off. On a device with a keyboard, the arrow keys or WASD move, Z undoes and R restarts.

Can I get stuck?

In twelve of the ninety-nine levels, yes — some positions have no route to the finish left, and the game tells you when you are in one so you are not walking a dead board without knowing it. In the other eighty-seven you cannot get stuck no matter what you do. Undo and restart are always available either way.

Levels

There are ninety-nine, grouped into chapters, and they open in order: the next unlocks when the one before it is solved. Every board was solved by computer before it shipped, so there is no level in the game that cannot be finished.

My progress disappeared

The save lives on the device only. It is not backed up to any server, because the app has no server. Deleting the app deletes it, and a new device starts from the first level. There is no account to sign into and no way for me to restore a save remotely.

One thing worth knowing: the app does not take your word for a solved level. It stores the sequence of directions you used and replays it to check, so a level counts as solved only while that sequence still finishes it. If a level went back to unsolved, write in and I will look at the case.

The name

The app is listed as Beraber and calls itself HERD on screen — same game, and the change of name did not touch anyone's saved progress.

How do I erase everything?

Delete the app. That removes the saved solutions and everything with them. Reinstalling gives you a fresh start from the first level.
