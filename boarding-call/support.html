Boarding Call — Support

Updated: 9 November 2026

Have a problem, a question or a suggestion? Write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and the game version (Settings → Privacy shows it).

Common questions

How do I play?

Select a passenger ticket and tap Board. Each traveller already has a seat, and you choose the order. Rear rows and window seats first usually saves time. Hold parks a passenger so you can see who is next. The clock stops once everyone has boarded.

Can I play without a timer?

Yes. Choose Take your time before a flight starts.

How do I get more coins?

Win flights. Every win pays, replays too, and longer flights pay more. Building at an airport raises what its flights pay. The free daily gift adds more.

I bought something and don't see it.

Open the shop (tap the coin counter) and tap Restore purchases. The Airport Pass, bundles and liveries come back on the same Apple ID. Coin packs and the coin jar are consumable.

The game runs slowly.

Turn on Battery saver in Settings and close other demanding apps.
