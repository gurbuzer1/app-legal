PRIVACY POLICY

Boarding Call

Effective: 9 November 2026

Boarding Call is a boarding puzzle with an airport to build. It has no account and no server, so this page is short.

Your game progress

Flight results, coins, airport buildings, paints and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes this progress. We do not receive it.

Purchases

Coin packs, bundles, the coin jar, the Airport Pass and liveries are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we receive no card or payment details. The game keeps a record of completed purchases on your device, so that each purchase is applied once and can be restored. Tap Restore purchases in the shop to bring back the Airport Pass, bundles and liveries on the same Apple ID.

Ads and analytics

There are none. This version of the game shows no ads, uses no analytics and does not track you.

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information, and we do not sell data.

Children

Boarding Call is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
