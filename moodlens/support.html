Support

MoodLens · last updated 23 August 2026

Anything at all — an insight that looks wrong, a streak that reset when it should not have, an entry you cannot find, a crash — write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. Telling me what you did just before it happened is usually enough for me to reproduce it.

Where are my entries kept?

On your phone, in the app's own storage, and nowhere else. No account, no cloud, no copy anywhere I could read. That is the point of the app — but it has one consequence worth saying plainly: if you delete the app or lose the phone, the entries go with it. Use Settings → Export Mood Data (CSV) if you want a copy you keep yourself.

Is anything locked behind a purchase?

No. There is nothing for sale in this version, so everything is unlocked: your whole history on the Insights tab, the calendar, the journal, and CSV export. If premium is ever offered you will be asked first, and nothing you already have will be taken away.

How is the "best weekday" worked out?

Each mood has a value, the app averages every entry you logged on each weekday, and the highest and lowest averages are the best and worst days. It is arithmetic on your own entries — not a prediction and not an opinion about you. A weekday you have never logged is left out rather than counted as neutral, which is why a single Monday can be your "best day" early on.

My streak reset and I did check in

A streak counts consecutive days, not entries: two entries on the same day count once, and a missed day breaks it. A streak that ended a week ago is shown as ended, not as still running. If your calendar shows an unbroken run of days and the streak still disagrees, that is a bug — please write to me.

Does it need internet?

No, and it never will. MoodLens makes no network request at all. Aeroplane mode changes nothing.

Is there an AI reading my journal?

No. Nothing you write is sent anywhere or fed to any model. Everything on the Insights tab is counted and averaged on your own phone.

Is this therapy?

No. MoodLens is a private notebook with some arithmetic on top. It is not a diagnosis and not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you are struggling, please talk to someone who can help.
