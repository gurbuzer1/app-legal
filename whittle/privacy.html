Privacy

Whittle · last updated 22 August 2026

Whittle collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it. The app makes no network requests at all. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores

Four numbers, on your own device, in the storage iOS gives the app for its own use: how many boards you have finished, how many you cleared, how many of those were without a mistake, and how many wrong moves you have made in total. That is the whole of it. Nothing in it identifies you, none of it is transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app removes it. Boards themselves are generated as you play and are never written down.

Where the facts come from

Every clue is a statement about a real country, taken from open data that ships inside the app: names, capitals, regions, areas, land borders and currencies from the mledoze/countries dataset, under the ODC Open Database License, and populations from World Bank indicator SP.POP.TOTL. Nothing is fetched while you play — the table is compiled into the app before it ships, which is why the game works with the aeroplane switch on.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for children. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no browser, no external links inside the app, and nothing to buy. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your device: deleting the app deletes all of it.
