SkillQuest — Support

Updated: 27 July 2026

Have a problem, a question or a suggestion? Write directly to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. Replies usually arrive within a few business days. If you are reporting a bug, including your device model and iOS version gets it fixed much faster.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. SkillQuest keeps your progress, streak and guild membership on your account, so it needs an email address and a password.

Does SkillQuest cost anything?

No. Version 1.0 is free and has no subscription or in-app purchases.

Someone is behaving badly in a guild. What do I do?

Guild chat is between real people. If you see abusive or inappropriate messages, email the address above with the guild name and roughly when it happened, and it will be dealt with.

I forgot my password.

Use the password reset option on the sign-in screen. If the reset email does not arrive, write to the address above from the same email address you signed up with.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email the address above from your account's email address and ask for deletion. Your account, progress and messages are removed; deleting the app alone does not remove server-side data.
