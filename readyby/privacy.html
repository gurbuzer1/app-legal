Privacy

ReadyBy · last updated 23 August 2026

ReadyBy collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it. The app makes no network request at all. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores

One thing: your presets. A preset is the list of small steps between deciding to go and actually being out of the door — "locker / change, 8 min", "security screening, 20 min" — for each kind of event. When you edit one, the edited list is written to the storage iOS gives the app for its own use, under a single key, so the app is useful the second time you open it.

Nothing else is kept. The event time, the travel minutes and the extra buffer you type into the calculator exist only while the screen is open and are gone when the app closes. An earlier version of this page also claimed the travel time and buffer were saved; they were not, and are not. Nothing stored identifies you, none of it is transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app removes it.

Permissions the app does not ask for

None — literally none. Info.plist contains no usage-description key of any kind, which is the only way an iOS app can request access to anything. In particular:

  • No calendar access. The example day on the main screen is four fixed events run through the same calculator you are using. It is not your diary and the app has no way to read your diary.
  • No location access. You type the travel time in. ReadyBy does not look it up and does not know where you are or where you are going.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, motion or notification access.

What the app does not record about you

ReadyBy does not measure how long you actually took. It has no history, so it can never tell you your "average drift" or how many trips you have made. An earlier build displayed exactly those figures — invented ones, in the second person, on a first launch — and they are gone. The figures on the "Where your time goes" screen are computed from your presets and nothing else.

Third parties

None. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend, no crash reporting service. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for children. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no browser, nothing to buy, and no external link inside the app. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your device: deleting the app deletes all of it.

Changes

If a future version ever collects something — a reminder that needs notification permission, for instance — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than buried.
