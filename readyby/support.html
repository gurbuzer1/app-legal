What it does

Maps tells you when you will arrive. ReadyBy tells you when to start getting ready — the last moment you can still act on. It works backwards from the event through your buffer, your journey, and the time you actually take to get out of the door.

Why are there three times?

Because they are three different decisions.

  • Start at — put the phone down and begin.
  • Leave by — be at the door.
  • Arrive by — be there, buffer still in hand.

"Start at" leads, because by the time "leave by" matters the decision has already been made for you.

I changed my setup time and only the first number moved

That is correct, and it is the fix rather than the bug. Getting ready happens at home, before you leave, so it can only move when you have to start — it cannot change when you arrive. An earlier version did move the wrong numbers: the badge the app is named after simply echoed the event time you had typed in, so whatever you changed, the number never moved. That is why there is now a test suite guarding the ordering.

Can it read my calendar?

No, and it never asks to. The example day on the main screen is four fixed events run through the same calculator you are using, so it can never show a time the app would not actually compute. It is a worked example, not your diary.

Does it know my travel time automatically?

No. You type it. The app does not use your location and does not contact a maps service — it makes no network request at all.

Where do my presets live?

On this device only. They do not follow you to a new phone, there is no backup, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Does it tell me how long I really took?

No. ReadyBy does not record what actually happened, so it never claims to know your habits. The "Where your time goes" screen is arithmetic on the presets you have edited — change a step and every figure on it changes with you.

Does it work offline?

Completely. ReadyBy never makes a network request, so aeroplane mode changes nothing.
