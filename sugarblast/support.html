Write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. A real person reads it. Tell me your device, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong — that is usually enough to find the problem.

The game

Swap two neighbouring sweets to line up three or more of a colour. Matching five sweets at once makes a colour bomb; four in a row makes a striped sweet; an L or a T makes a wrapped one. There are thirty levels. Each has a move limit and three score targets, so beating a level and three-starring it are two different achievements.

Lives

You hold up to five lives. Starting a level spends one. A spent life comes back twenty minutes later, and they keep coming back until you are at five again — the countdown on the home screen is the wait for the next one.

Waiting is the only way to get a life back. There is nothing in the shop that refills them and no way to buy one; if some text in the game suggests otherwise, the text is wrong and the shop is right.

Coins and boosters

Coins come from playing: stars at the end of a level, daily quests, achievements, and the daily-login reward. They cannot be bought — the game has no in-app purchases at all.

The shop sells five boosters and nothing else. +5 Moves (50), Color Bomb (75) and Striped Row (75) are chosen before a level starts; Hammer (100) and Shuffle (50) are used during one.

Daily quests and login

Three quests are drawn each day and reset with the calendar day on your device. The login reward runs on a seven-day ladder — the day only advances when the date moves forward, so changing your clock will not pay you twice.

My progress disappeared

The save lives on the device only. It is not backed up to any server, because the game has no server. Deleting the app deletes the save, and a new device starts from level one. There is no account to sign into and no way for me to restore a save remotely.

If progress vanished without the app being deleted, please write — include which level you had reached, and I will look at it.

How do I erase everything?

Delete the app. That removes the single saved record and everything in it. Reinstalling gives you a fresh start.

Privacy

The game collects nothing and sends nothing. The full statement is at https://gurbuzer1.github.io/app-legal/sugarblast/privacy.html.
