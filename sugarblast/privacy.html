Privacy

Sugar Blast: Match & Pop · last updated 23 August 2026

Sugar Blast collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it. The game makes no network requests. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores

One saved game, on your own device, in the storage iOS gives the app for its own use. It is a single record and it holds only the state of your play:

  • which levels you have beaten, the best score on each, and the stars that score earns;
  • your coin balance, your remaining lives, and the moment the life timer last ticked;
  • how many of each booster you own;
  • your daily-login day and the date it was last claimed;
  • today's three quests and how far along each one is;
  • your current and best win streak, and eight lifetime counters — matches made, big cascades, special candies created, colour bombs, coins earned, wins, best cascade, best streak;
  • which achievements have unlocked.

That is the whole of it. Nothing in it identifies you, none of it is transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app removes it. The boards themselves are generated as you play and are never written down.

Money

There are no in-app purchases and no way to spend real money in this game. Coins are earned by playing — from stars at the end of a level, from daily quests, from achievements, and from the daily-login reward — and the only thing they buy is one of the five boosters in the shop. The app contains no purchasing code and links no purchasing framework, so there is nothing to restore, nothing to bill, and no payment detail for the game to see.

Lives are not for sale either. You start with five, playing a level spends one, and one comes back every twenty minutes until you are full again. Waiting is the only way to refill them.

Permissions

The game never asks for the microphone, the camera, your location, your photos, your contacts, or permission to send notifications — no part of the code requests any of them, so iOS never puts the question to you. The only device feature it touches is the haptic engine: the phone taps back when you make a match. That needs no permission and records nothing.

Children

The game is safe for children. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no browser, no link that leaves the app, and nothing to buy. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your device: deleting the app deletes all of it.
