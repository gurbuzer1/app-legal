Privacy

Lantern Keeper · last updated 1 October 2026

Lantern Keeper collects nothing and sends nothing. The game makes no network requests while you play. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the game stores

Your progress, on your own device only, in the storage the app is given for its own use: embers and stars you have earned, which nights you have won and how many stars each, which parts of the island you have restored, which keepers you have unlocked, the upgrades you bought, your daily-reward streak and today's quest progress, your best time in the Long Night, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics).

None of it identifies you. It never leaves the device.

Deleting it

Settings → Reset progress erases everything. Deleting the app erases it too.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if purchases are added), this page will say so before the update ships.
