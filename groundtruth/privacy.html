Privacy

Ground Truth · last updated 23 August 2026

Ground Truth collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it. The app makes no network request at all. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores

One entry, on your own device, in the storage the app is given for its own use. It is written under the key groundtruth.save.v1 and holds exactly three things:

  • which puzzles you have solved, and how many attempts each one took;
  • which puzzle you opened last, so that Continue goes somewhere sensible;
  • one true-or-false flag recording whether the one-time unlock has been bought.

That is the whole list. Nothing in it identifies you, none of it is transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app removes it. There is no settings screen and no preference file, because the app has no sound, no settings and nothing to configure.

The purchase

Ground Truth sells one thing: a single, one-time unlock of the puzzles after the free opening chapter. It is not a subscription and it does not renew. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app the localised price and whether the unlock is owned; that is everything the app learns. Your name, your payment details and your Apple Account are never visible to it, and no receipt is sent anywhere by us because there is nowhere to send one.

Where the puzzles come from

Every puzzle, source card, wrong-answer explanation and closing line is written by hand and compiled into the app before it ships. Nothing you tap is sent to a language model, and no puzzle is generated while you play. The app behaves identically with the aeroplane switch on, because it never had a connection to lose.

Permissions

None are requested, because none are used. The app asks for no location, no contacts, no photos, no camera, no microphone, no notifications, and no tracking permission. There is no permission prompt anywhere in it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no browser and no advertising. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your device: deleting the app deletes all of it.

Changes

If a future version ever collects or sends anything, this page will say so plainly and will be updated before that version reaches the store.
