Anything at all — a puzzle you believe has a second answer, an explanation that did not explain, a purchase that did not restore — write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. The puzzle's heading and what you picked is enough for me to find it.

What the game is

Ninety puzzles across nine chapters, ten to a chapter, in three kinds:

  • Prompt Repair — you are shown what the output has to be and a pile of constraints. Pick the set that gets there, and nothing more.
  • Spot the Fabrication — a confident answer broken into claims, and the sources it was supposedly built from. Mark what the sources do not actually support.
  • Build the Pipeline — chain the steps that meet the brief inside a budget, inside a time limit, and without the data leaving the building.

Every chapter carries all three, so the free opening chapter is a fair sample of the whole game rather than ten of the easiest kind.

I think a puzzle has more than one right answer

Every puzzle is checked by brute force before it ships: the test suite enumerates the whole answer space of all ninety and fails the build if any of them has two solutions or none. If one still feels ambiguous, that is a bug worth having — send it.

My prompt met every requirement and was still marked wrong

Because a correct prompt is the smallest one that meets them. A constraint you could delete without changing the output is dead weight, and the game names it:

One constraint is dead weight — "sound confident". Confidence is the default failure mode, not a goal.

The same is true in the other direction: two instructions that quietly fight each other are reported as a conflict, with both halves named, rather than as "try again".

What do I get if I pay?

The first chapter is free and covers all three kinds of puzzle. One payment unlocks the remaining eighty puzzles permanently. There is no subscription, no renewal, and nothing else to buy afterwards.

I paid and the puzzles are locked again

Use Restore a previous purchase on the unlock sheet — it is reachable without paying anything. The unlock follows your Apple Account, so a new phone or a reinstall gets it back. Puzzle progress is a different thing: it is stored on the device only, so it does not travel to a new phone. Nothing you have paid for is ever taken away by a failed store lookup — a store that cannot be reached leaves everything exactly as it was.

Does it work offline?

Completely, and it always will. The app makes no network request of any kind. Aeroplane mode changes nothing about how it behaves.

Are the puzzles written by AI?

No. Every puzzle, source card, wrong-answer explanation and closing line is written by hand and compiled into the app before it ships. Nothing you tap is sent to a model, and nothing is generated while you play.

Will the puzzles go out of date?

They are written to avoid it. Nothing depends on a particular model, price or context size — the lessons are about how to constrain output and how to check work, and neither expires when a model does.

What happens when I finish?

The last puzzle ends on a closing screen with what you solved and how many you took on the first attempt, and it names the first puzzle you skipped if you left any. Nothing expires and nothing needs a connection, so the whole game stays where you left it.
