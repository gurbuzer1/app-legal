Support

Sealed · last updated 23 August 2026

Anything at all — a board that looked wrong, an offer you disagree with, a crash — write to gurbuzer1@gmail.com.

How to play

Pick one of the eighteen boxes and keep it. Open the others a few at a time. After each round you are offered a sum for your box; take it, or refuse and keep opening. If you refuse every offer, you open your own box and are paid whatever it holds.

Is the app deciding what is in my box as I go?

No, and this is the question the game is built to answer rather than deflect. The board is dealt once, before you touch anything, and the arrangement is never written to again.

That is checked rather than claimed. The same board is played through three times in the test suite — opening the boxes in ascending order, in descending order, and at random — and the contents have to come out identical every time. Something deciding on the fly would have to agree with itself across three different orders, which is exactly what it could not do.

Where does the offer come from?

It is a fixed share of what your box is worth on average across everything still sealed: 34% in the first round, then 46, 58, 70, 82, 92, and the full amount in the last. That table is in the app and in the source.

It depends on the round and on what is left, and on nothing else — not on how well you are doing, not on what you refused before, not on a hidden counter. The same board in the same round always offers the same number.

Why show me the fair value?

Because withholding it would be the only unfair thing in the game. On television a person in a booth produces the offer and the tension comes from not knowing how far below fair it is — that works because a human being is visibly deciding. In an app the same opacity is just an unfalsifiable claim, so the screen prints both numbers and lets you decide with them.

Is any of this real money?

No. They are points, and deliberately not any country's currency — this format travels the world precisely because the mechanic carries no language and no money. There is nothing to buy in this app and nothing to win.

Does it need a connection?

No, and it never will. Everything happens on your phone. Aeroplane mode changes nothing.

Privacy

Nothing is collected. The full statement is on the privacy page.

Do I get the same board every time I open the app?

No. Until 23 August 2026 you did — the app opened on a fixed seed, so the very first board of every session was the same arrangement, and only pressing "New board" moved it on. The opening seed is now drawn per launch. Deals are still reproducible from a seed, which is what lets a board be recovered from a bug report; what changed is that the app no longer starts from the same one.

What is the road under my record?

Right calls — the boards where what you kept beat what you turned down. Not boards played, which would measure attendance, and not the amount kept, which would measure luck. The milestones are at 1, 5, 15, 40 and 80, and the line beneath always carries how many boards those calls came from: someone taking offers at random is right about half the time, so a bare count would climb on its own.
