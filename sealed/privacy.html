Privacy

Sealed · last updated 23 August 2026

Sealed collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it. The app makes no network requests at all. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could add one.

What the app stores

Four numbers, on your own device, in the storage iOS gives the app for its own use: how many boards you have played, the most you have kept from one, how many boards you ended by selling, and how many times what you kept beat what you turned down. That is the whole of it. Nothing in it identifies you, none of it is transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app removes it.

The board itself is never written down, and that is deliberate rather than incidental: the promise of this game is that the arrangement is fixed at deal time and never touched again, and a saved board would be a file that could be edited between one session and the next.

There is no money here

The values on the board are points. Nothing in this app can be bought, sold, wagered or withdrawn, and no payment method is ever involved. The ladder runs to 750,000 because that is a satisfying number, not because it is a sum.

The board is not watching you

Worth saying plainly, because it is the thing people reasonably wonder about a game like this: the arrangement of the boxes is decided before you touch anything and is never changed afterwards. It does not respond to how you are playing, and neither does the offer — that is a fixed share of what is left, published on the app's own first screen. None of it involves anything leaving your phone, because nothing leaves your phone.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for children. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no browser, no external links inside the app, and nothing to buy. Since nothing is collected from anybody, nothing is collected from children either.

Third parties

None. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend. The app has no server component, so there is no operator to whom your data could be disclosed and no breach that could expose it.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA — apply to data a company holds about you. No such data exists here. Everything the app has ever written is on your device: deleting the app deletes all of it.
