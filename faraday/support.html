Have a problem, a question or a suggestion? Write directly to gurbuzer1@gmail.com. Replies usually arrive within a few business days. If you are reporting a bug, including your device model and iOS version gets it fixed much faster.

Common questions

What does Faraday actually do?

It reads your calendar on the device, notices when you are about to lose signal — a flight, the daily metro dead zone — and downloads podcast episodes and a short briefing while you still have wifi, so they are already on your phone when you go offline.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or password.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to download. The whole point is that everything already on your phone plays with the signal off.

Why does it ask for calendar access?

That is how it spots your trips. Your events are read on the device and never leave it — see the privacy policy. You can decline and still add trips by hand.

Can it download from YouTube, Netflix or Spotify?

No, and it does not pretend to. For those it gives you a checklist that opens each app so you can save things there yourself.

Some talks say "Sample" — why?

Those are example entries shown to illustrate the pack. They have no licensed audio source wired in yet, so they are not downloaded. Podcast episodes are real and do download.

Nothing downloads overnight — what is wrong?

Overnight prefetch is scheduled by iOS, which runs it when the phone has wifi and charge. Low Power Mode blocks it; the Setup screen will say so when it does.

How do I delete my data?

Clear the downloaded pack from the Setup screen, or delete the app to remove everything. Nothing is stored on a server, so there is nothing for us to delete.
