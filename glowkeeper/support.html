Glowkeeper

Stand still to shine, move to slip away. Your lantern grows brighter when you stand still: it burns the night creatures, but bright light draws them in. Move and the light fades, they lose you, and you can scoop up embers that refill your lamp oil.

Common questions

My progress is gone. Progress is stored only on your device; deleting the app or using Reset progress erases it.

No sound. Check Settings → Sound effects / Music and your device's silent switch.

The game feels slow. Settings → Graphics → Battery renders fewer pixels.
